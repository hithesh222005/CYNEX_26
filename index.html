🕵️ Cyber Crime Investigation

Event Type Technical
Venue Lab Centre - 04
Date 26 Sept 2026
Level National

Event Overview

Cyber Crime Investigation is a cutting-edge technical event designed to challenge participants to analyze cyber incidents, uncover digital clues, identify attack techniques, and investigate cybercrime scenarios with the precision of a seasoned digital detective.

This event tests your ability to think like a cybersecurity professional, analyzing evidence, tracing attack patterns, and solving complex digital mysteries. Every clue matters. Every log tells a story.

What You'll Do

Participants will work with investigation-based challenges involving:

  • Digital Forensics & Evidence Analysis - Extract and analyze data from digital systems
  • Network Forensics & Cyber Attack Investigation - Trace attack paths and identify threat actors
  • Clue-Based Cybercrime Challenges - Solve complex cybercrime scenarios using logical reasoning
  • Log Analysis & System Artifacts - Investigate system logs for suspicious activities
  • Malware Analysis Basics - Identify and classify malicious code signatures
  • Real-World Cybersecurity Scenarios - Apply practical cybersecurity skills

Skills You'll Need

This event tests and develops:

  • Analytical & Logical Thinking - Break down complex problems
  • Technical Knowledge - Understanding of cybersecurity concepts
  • Evidence Collection & Analysis - Proper forensic procedures
  • Problem-Solving Ability - Quick thinking under pressure
  • Attention to Detail - Spotting critical clues
  • Report Writing - Documenting your findings professionally

Who Can Participate?

Open to engineering and technology students with an interest in:

  • Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking
  • Digital Forensics & Incident Response
  • Network Security & Administration
  • Information Security

Event Rules & Guidelines

  • Teams of 2-3 members per group
  • Time limit: 2-3 hours per round
  • No external internet access during the event
  • Provided tools and utilities only
  • Judging based on accuracy, speed, and documentation quality

How to Prepare?

  • Learn basic Linux commands and file systems
  • Understand network concepts (IP, TCP/UDP, DNS)
  • Practice with forensic tools like FTK, EnCase, or Volatility
  • Study real cyber incident case studies
  • Develop pattern recognition skills

Ready to Hunt the Threats?

Challenge yourself with real-world cybercrime scenarios. Prove your investigative skills and become a cyber detective.

🧠 Memory Matrix

Event Type Technical
Venue Lab Centre - 04
Date 26 Sept 2026
Level National

Event Overview

Memory Matrix is a fast-paced technical event designed to challenge participants to observe, remember, and accurately recall patterns, symbols, numbers, sequences, and technical information under intense time pressure.

This event tests the peak performance of your cognitive abilities - how quickly you can process information, how accurately you can retain details, and how effectively you can recall them when it matters most.

What You'll Face

Participants will encounter:

  • Pattern & Sequence Recognition - Visual and logical patterns
  • Memory & Concentration Challenges - Increasing difficulty levels
  • Speed & Accuracy Tests - Race against the clock
  • Number and Symbol Recall - Remember complex sequences
  • Logical Thinking & Problem Solving - Connect the dots
  • Progressive Difficulty - Each round becomes harder

Round Structure

  • Round 1: Basic Pattern Recognition (Easy)
  • Round 2: Complex Sequences & Symbols (Medium)
  • Round 3: Rapid-Fire Recall Challenge (Hard)
  • Round 4: Final Boss Round (Extreme)

Skills Tested

This event evaluates:

  • Short-term Memory Capacity - How much you can hold
  • Observation Skills - Spotting the details others miss
  • Concentration Under Pressure - Staying focused
  • Quick Decision Making - Think fast, act faster
  • Logical Reasoning - Making sense of patterns
  • Mental Stamina - Sustaining peak performance

Who Can Participate?

Open to all engineering and technology students who are ready to:

  • Challenge their memory and observation skills
  • Test their concentration and focus
  • Push their cognitive performance limits
  • Compete at a national level

Tips to Prepare

  • Play brain games and memory apps regularly
  • Practice pattern recognition exercises
  • Improve concentration through meditation
  • Take timed mock tests to build speed
  • Learn mnemonic techniques for quick recall
  • Practice visual memory exercises

Event Rules

  • Individual competition
  • Strict time limits per round
  • No external aids or communication devices
  • Electronic device usage strictly prohibited
  • Judging based on accuracy and speed

Test Your Mental Prowess

Can you remember everything you see? Can you spot patterns others miss? Find out at Memory Matrix!

🎨 Reverse Pictionary

Event Type Non-Technical
Venue S2
Date 26 Sept 2026
Level National

Event Overview

Reverse Pictionary is a fun and exciting non-technical event where participants turn words, concepts, or clues into creative drawings while their teammates try to guess them within a limited time. It's a celebration of creativity, communication, and teamwork!

Think you can draw? Think your team can guess? This event proves that sometimes the best ideas come from the wildest sketches!

How It Works

  • Your team gets a word or concept
  • You have 60 seconds to draw it (without writing letters or numbers)
  • Your teammates try to guess what you're drawing
  • Points awarded for correct guesses and speed
  • Move through multiple rounds with increasing difficulty

Event Highlights

What makes this event amazing:

  • Creative Drawing Challenges - Express yourself through art
  • Quick Thinking & Guessing - Be ready for anything
  • Teamwork & Communication - Communicate without words
  • Time-Based Challenges - Race against the clock
  • Fun & Engaging Rounds - Laugh, sketch, and win!
  • Theme-Based Categories - Cybersecurity, movies, concepts, and more

Round Structure

  • Round 1: Easy Mode (Common objects and concepts)
  • Round 2: Medium Mode (Abstract ideas and technical terms)
  • Round 3: Hard Mode (Complex cybersecurity concepts)
  • Final Round: Speed Round (Multiple items in 30 seconds)

Scoring System

  • Correct guess before time ends: +10 points
  • Guessed in first 30 seconds: +5 bonus points
  • Multiple items in speed round: Points per correct answer
  • Highest team score wins the event

Who Can Participate?

Open to all engineering and technology students. No technical knowledge required!

  • Team size: 3-4 members per team
  • All members participate in guessing
  • One drawer per round (rotates)
  • Mix of artistic and non-artistic people welcome

Drawing Tips

  • Focus on shapes and symbols, not perfect art
  • Use colors strategically to hint at the concept
  • Draw large and clear - avoid tiny details
  • Think of associations - what makes people think of your concept?
  • Don't overthink - simplicity often works better
  • Use arrows and lines to show action

Guessing Tips

  • Watch the drawing unfold - context matters
  • Call out related words to narrow it down
  • Be loud and confident in your guesses
  • Help teammates with ideas
  • Think laterally - the obvious answer isn't always right

Event Rules

  • No writing letters, numbers, or words on paper
  • No speaking hints (except in guessing phase)
  • Drawer cannot speak during drawing time
  • All drawings must be original (no tracing)
  • Strict time limits enforced
  • Judges' decisions are final

Ready to Sketch Your Way to Victory?

Grab your team, sharpen your creative skills, and come draw, guess, and laugh at Reverse Pictionary!
